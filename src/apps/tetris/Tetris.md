# Tetris

![tetris](tetris.png)

A classic game, implemented for the Pixelino. Place the falling tetrominos into order but leave no space. Completely filled rows will disappear. With every full row the game gets a little faster.

**Author:** Fritz Feichtinger

**Date:** 2026-10-24

## Controls
* Left:   move left
* Right:  move right
* Down:   move down
* Up:     rotate clockwise
* A:      rotate clockwise
* B:      rotate counter-clockwise
* Select: pause / resume

## Tips
* On the 8x8 display it gets tight quickly. Try to keep your heap low. Think fast and be precise. Errors are hard to fix later.

* All 7 types of tetrominos appear with the same propability. However they are not selected purely random but will come in rounds. If you closely observe the appearing tetrominos you can make your predictions what comes next.
